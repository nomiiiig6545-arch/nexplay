Everything in one place

Three players and a set of useful tools, built to feel fast and stay out of your way.

Video player

A fast, gesture-driven player for every format your phone can decode.

  • Double-tap to skip, slide for brightness and volume, hold to speed up
  • Playback speed, aspect-ratio (Fit) modes, night mode, mirror mode, screenshot and screen lock
  • Audio tracks, subtitles, bookmarks and cutting a clip
  • A Now Playing queue you can reorder, and a one-tap rotate button
  • Continue from where you stopped; Recently Played and Favorites
  • Picture-in-Picture when you leave the app

Music player

A beautiful music player with a spinning record and live spectrum.

  • Songs, Albums, Artists, Folders, Playlists and Favorites
  • Equalizer, lyrics, sleep timer, shuffle, repeat and A-B repeat
  • Speed control
  • Plays in the background with notification and a mini player
  • Home-screen widget and “set as ringtone”

Photos & editor

Browse every album and fix your pictures without leaving the app.

  • Albums, Favorites and a smooth full-screen viewer
  • Built-in photo editor
  • Optional AI enhancement with your own Gemini key
  • Share, delete, or move to the private vault

Private Folder

Lock videos, songs and photos behind a PIN or your fingerprint.

  • Files are hidden in the app’s private storage, with the file list encrypted
  • Reset a forgotten PIN with a security question, recovery key or email
  • Plays vault videos and music inside the vault

Status Saver

Save WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business statuses you have viewed.

  • Pick the status folder yourself with Android’s own folder picker
  • No extra storage permission needed
  • Play statuses right inside Prime Media, then save the ones you want

Video to Audio

Turn any video into a song-ready audio file in a few taps.

  • MP3 (192 kbps) or lossless M4A
  • Saved to Music/Prime Media
  • Or play a video as audio only, with a Back to video button

Networks

Reach files on your NAS or PC without copying them first.

  • FTP, FTPS, SFTP and SMB
  • Browse folders, tap a file to download it
  • Server passwords are stored encrypted on your phone

Cloud Drive

Sign in to Dropbox, browse your folders, and play your videos and songs straight from the cloud.

  • Tap a video or song to play it without downloading, or download it to Download/Prime Media
  • Filter by All, Video or Audio
  • Read-only access to your files
  • Login stored encrypted; sign out any time

Recycle Bin & Media Manager

Delete with confidence and see where your space went.

  • Deleted videos wait 30 days in the Recycle Bin (Android 11+)
  • See storage used by videos, music and images
  • Find your largest and newest videos

Themes & languages

Make it yours: pick a colour theme and your language.

  • Light and dark themes with multiple accent colours
  • English, Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, Bengali, Indonesian and Turkish

Cast to TV

Send a video from your phone to a Chromecast or Google TV.

  • Connect through your own Wi-Fi; nothing is uploaded to the internet
  • Pause, seek and stop from your phone
  • “Convert & cast” makes a temporary TV-friendly copy for formats like MKV or HEVC
  • Private Folder videos cannot be cast

Make video smaller

Turn a big video into a small copy that is easy to share or to play on a small phone.

  • 720p, 480p, 360p, 240p or 144p, or “fit in” 10, 25, 50 or 100 MB
  • Choose the quality and sound, and compress many videos at once
  • Runs in the background with a progress notification and Cancel
  • Your original is never touched; the copy shows how much space you saved

Play from link

Paste a web address of a video and watch it in Prime Media.

  • Opens the link directly in the player
  • Used only when you start it

Backup, history & streaks

Keep your settings safe and see how you use the app.

  • Back up and restore your settings, favorites and playlists as a file
  • Watch history and Continue Watching, which you can clear any time
  • Watch streaks and achievements

Quick answers

Is Prime Media really free?

Yes. Prime Media is free and has no ads.

Which permissions does it need, and why?

Access to your videos, music and photos to show them; notifications and a media service so music keeps playing with the screen off; and an optional microphone permission that only drives the live music visualizer (nothing is recorded). Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Does it work with WhatsApp statuses?

Yes. View a status in WhatsApp, then open Me → Status Saver, choose WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, pick its status folder once, and tap the download icon. Prime Media is not affiliated with WhatsApp.

I forgot my vault PIN.

Tap Forgot PIN? in the vault. You can reset it with your security question, a recovery key, or an email reset link, if you set one up. Your vault files stay. If you have none of these, the vault cannot be opened and we cannot recover it, so set up recovery and back up anything important.

Can I play videos on my TV?

Yes. Connect your phone and Chromecast or Google TV to the same Wi-Fi, then tap ⋮ → Cast in the player. The video goes from your phone to the TV over your own network.

Can I make a large video smaller?

Yes. Open the video’s menu and tap Make smaller, or use Me → Video Compress. You get a new, smaller copy and your original stays as it is.

Can I play my Dropbox videos without downloading them?

Yes. Open Me → Cloud Drive, sign in, and tap a video or song to play it directly.
