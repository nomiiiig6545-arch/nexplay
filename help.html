Help & FAQ

Answers to common questions about NexPlay.

How do I use the private vault?

Open Me → Private Folder and create a PIN. Then move videos, songs or photos into it from their menus. The vault locks when you leave the app.

I deleted a video by mistake.

Open Me → Recycle Bin (Android 11 and newer) and tap the restore icon within 30 days.

How do I save a WhatsApp status?

View the status in WhatsApp first. Then open Me → Status Saver, choose WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, pick its status folder once, and tap the download icon on a status. Tap a status to play it inside NexPlay. Swipe left or right to switch between Recent and Saved.

How do I get the audio of a video?

Open Me → Video to Audio, pick a video, then choose MP3 (converted, 192 kbps) or M4A (instant, no quality loss). The file is saved in Music/NexPlay. Videos with no audio track cannot be converted. You can also tap the headphone button in the video player to listen as audio only.

How do I connect to a server?

Open Me → Networks, tap +, choose FTP, FTPS, SFTP or SMB and enter the server details. For SMB the host is the computer or NAS, and you start by seeing its shares. Tap a file to download it to Download/NexPlay.

How do I use Cloud Drive?

Open Me → Cloud Drive and sign in with Dropbox. Browse your folders and tap a file to download it to Download/NexPlay. Use the sign-out icon at the top to disconnect.

How do I free up space?

Me → Media Manager shows how much space your videos, music and images use, plus your largest and newest videos.

How do I play a file from another app?

Tap the file in WhatsApp, Files or any app and choose NexPlay from the “Open with” list. Videos, songs and photos open right inside NexPlay.

The music keeps stopping in the background.

Some phones close background apps to save battery. In Android Settings, set NexPlay’s battery option to “Unrestricted” or “Don’t optimise”, and allow notifications.

Some videos or pictures are missing.

Make sure NexPlay has access to all your photos and videos (Android 14 may be set to “selected only”). Open Settings → Apps → NexPlay → Permissions and allow all, then pull down to refresh.
