Help & FAQ

Answers to common questions about Prime Media.

How do I use the private vault?

Open Me → Private Folder and create a PIN. Then move videos, songs or photos into it from their menus. The vault locks when you leave the app. Files you move in are stored inside Prime Media, so use the vault menu → Back up to a folder before uninstalling the app.

I forgot my vault PIN.

Tap Forgot PIN? on the vault screen and use your security question, your recovery key, or Email me a reset link. Then choose a new PIN; your vault files stay. To prepare, open the vault menu → Recover PIN by email, enter your email and send yourself the recovery key.

I deleted a video by mistake.

Open Me → Recycle Bin (Android 11 and newer) and tap the restore icon within 30 days.

How do I save a WhatsApp status?

View the status in WhatsApp first. Then open Me → Status Saver, choose WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, pick its status folder once, and tap the download icon on a status. Tap a status to play it inside Prime Media. Swipe left or right to switch between Recent and Saved.

How do I get the audio of a video?

Open Me → Video to Audio, pick a video, then choose MP3 (converted, 192 kbps) or M4A (instant, no quality loss). The file is saved in Music/Prime Media. Videos with no audio track cannot be converted. You can also tap the headphone button in the video player to listen as audio only.

How do I connect to a server?

Open Me → Networks, tap +, choose FTP, FTPS, SFTP or SMB and enter the server details. For SMB the host is the computer or NAS, and you start by seeing its shares. Tap a file to download it to Download/Prime Media.

How do I use Cloud Drive?

Open Me → Cloud Drive and sign in with Dropbox. Browse your folders. Tap a video or song to play it directly without downloading, or tap the download icon beside a file to save it to Download/Prime Media. Use the All / Video / Audio chips at the top to show only videos or songs. Use the sign-out icon at the top to disconnect. Prime Media can only read your Dropbox; it never changes or deletes anything there.

Cloud Drive shows “Nothing here yet” or an empty folder.

Dropbox may only let Prime Media see its own folder. Open the Dropbox app or dropbox.com, go to Apps → Prime Media and upload your videos or songs there. Then reopen Cloud Drive. Large videos play faster on a good Wi-Fi or 4G connection.

How do I make a big video smaller?

Open the video’s menu (⋮) and tap Make smaller, or open Me → Video Compress and tap + to pick videos. Choose a size (720p down to 144p) or “fit in” a size such as 25 MB, and set the quality and sound. You can leave the app: a notification shows the progress and has a Cancel button. The smaller copy is saved in Movies/Prime Media and listed under Completed with how much space you saved. Your original video is not touched. You can share the copy or delete it from the list, and the notification has a Delete original button if you want to remove the big one (Android will ask you to confirm).

How do I play a video on my TV (Cast)?

Connect your phone and the Chromecast / Google TV to the same Wi-Fi. In the player tap ⋮ → Cast and choose your TV. Use the controls on your phone to pause, seek and stop. If your TV cannot play the video format (for example MKV or HEVC), tap Convert & cast; Prime Media makes a temporary copy that is removed when you stop casting. Videos in the Private Folder cannot be cast.

What is the Now Playing list?

While a video from a folder is playing, tap ⋮ → Playing Queue to see the videos of that folder. Tap one to play it, drag the handle to reorder, or tap ✕ to remove it from the queue (the file itself is not deleted).

How do I free up space?

Me → Media Manager shows how much space your videos, music and images use, plus your largest and newest videos.

How do I play a file from another app?

Tap the file in WhatsApp, Files or any app and choose Prime Media from the “Open with” list. Videos, songs and photos open right inside Prime Media.

The music keeps stopping in the background.

Some phones close background apps to save battery. In Android Settings, set Prime Media’s battery option to “Unrestricted” or “Don’t optimise”, and allow notifications.

Some videos or pictures are missing.

Make sure Prime Media has access to all your photos and videos (Android 14 may be set to “selected only”). Open Settings → Apps → Prime Media → Permissions and allow all, then pull down to refresh.
