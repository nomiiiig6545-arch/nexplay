Summary

In short: your videos, music and photos stay on your phone. Prime Media has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer does not sell or share your personal data. Prime Media never uploads your files to the developer or to any server of ours. Data only leaves your phone when you start a feature that needs it: the email address you choose for PIN recovery, the Dropbox account you sign in to, a server you add, the optional Gemini photo enhancement, or a video you cast to a TV on your own Wi-Fi (see Internet use and third-party services).

This Privacy Policy explains what the Prime Media Android app (package name com.novaplay.videoplayer) accesses, how it uses that access, and the choices you have. It applies to the app and to this website.

What Prime Media accesses and why

Prime Media asks for the following Android permissions. You can withdraw any of them at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Prime Media → Permissions.

PermissionWhy it is needed
Photos and videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO; READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and older)To list and play your videos and show your photos. On Android 14 and newer you may choose to share only selected photos and videos (READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED).
Music and audio (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO)To list and play your songs.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)To show the playback notification and its controls, and the progress and result of “Make video smaller”.
Foreground service – media playback; Wake lockTo keep music playing when the screen is off or the app is in the background.
Foreground service – data sync (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_DATA_SYNC)Only while “Make video smaller” is working, so a long conversion keeps running with a visible notification (with a Cancel button) when you leave the app. It stops when the work is finished or cancelled.

Prime Media does not request “All files access” (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE), location, contacts, camera, SMS or phone permissions, and it cannot draw over other apps. Cast to TV needs no extra permission.

Information we collect

Prime Media does not collect, sell, rent or share personal information, and it contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. The developer runs no server that receives your files or usage.

One optional exception: if you set up PIN recovery by email, the email address you enter is processed by Google Firebase Authentication (see PIN recovery). If you never set this up, no personal information leaves your phone.

The following stays only on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app: your settings and theme, watch history and “continue watching” positions, favorites, playlists, saved server details, and the vault.

How your media is used

Your videos, music and photos are read on your device only to list, play, edit and display them. Thumbnails and temporary files are created on your device. Files opened with “Open with” from another app are played directly and are not copied or uploaded. When you cut a clip, extract audio or use “Make video smaller”, the new file is created on your phone and saved in your own storage (see Where files are saved).

Internet use and third-party services

Prime Media uses the internet only for things you start yourself. When you use them, the relevant third party receives data under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • PIN recovery by email (Google Firebase Authentication): only if you set it up and only when you tap “Email me a reset link”. See PIN recovery and Google’s privacy policy.
  • Cloud Drive (Dropbox): when you sign in, Prime Media talks to Dropbox to list your files, play them (streaming) and download them. Prime Media asks Dropbox only for permission to read your files and basic account information, and it cannot change, upload or delete anything in your Dropbox. When you tap a video or song, Dropbox gives Prime Media a temporary link that the player opens directly, so the file goes from Dropbox to your phone and not through any server of ours. The login token is stored encrypted on your phone and you can sign out at any time. See Dropbox’s privacy policy.
  • Networks (FTP, FTPS, SFTP, SMB): Prime Media connects only to servers you add. Server details, including passwords, are stored encrypted on your phone. Data goes directly between your phone and your server.
  • Play from link: if you paste a web address of a video, Prime Media opens that address directly, like a browser would. The website that hosts the video receives your request and your IP address under its own terms. Prime Media does not send the link to the developer.
  • Cast to TV (Google Cast): only when you tap Cast in the player. Prime Media uses Google’s Cast framework (part of Google Play services) to find Cast-enabled TVs and speakers on your local Wi-Fi. For the video you pick, your phone briefly acts as a small server on your local network so the TV can fetch that one file. The video is not uploaded to the internet. Private Folder (vault) videos cannot be cast. See Cast to TV and Google’s privacy policy.
  • Remove Background: Prime Media finds the main person or object in a picture on your phone using Google ML Kit. The picture is not uploaded. The first time, Google Play services may download a small model to your phone, which needs the internet once. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Optional photo enhancement (Google Gemini): if you choose to use it, the photo you select is sent to Google’s Gemini service using your own API key. It is not used unless you start it. See Google’s privacy policy.

Private Folder (vault)

When you move a video, song or photo to the vault, Prime Media copies it into a private folder inside the app that other apps and the gallery cannot see, and then removes the original. The list of vault files and their real names is stored encrypted on your phone. Your PIN is stored only as a salted hash, never as the PIN itself. Fingerprint unlock uses Android’s own system; Prime Media never receives your fingerprint.

Important: vault files live inside Prime Media’s storage. If you uninstall the app or clear its data they are deleted and we cannot get them back. Use Vault → menu → Back up to a folder to keep a copy. The vault hides your files from other people who use the phone’s gallery or file manager; it does not replace your phone’s own lock screen.

PIN recovery

If you forget your vault PIN you can reset it and your vault files stay. You can set up any of these:

  • Security question. Only a salted hash of your answer is stored, on your phone.
  • Recovery key. Prime Media makes a random key, stores only a salted hash of it on your phone, and opens your own email app so you can send the key to yourself. Prime Media does not send this email.
  • Email reset link. If you enter an email address for recovery, the address is stored on your phone and Prime Media creates an account for that address with Google Firebase Authentication (the account has a random password nobody knows). When you tap “Email me a reset link”, Firebase sends a message to that address so you can prove you own it. The only information sent to Firebase is your email address and the technical data every internet request carries (such as your IP address). No vault files, file names, PIN or media are ever sent. Firebase is operated by Google and covered by Google’s privacy policy.

Deleting your recovery email: email nomiig654534@gmail.com from the same address and we will delete the Firebase account. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone.

Status Saver

When you tap WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, Android’s own folder picker opens and you choose that app’s status folder. Prime Media reads only that folder, plays statuses inside the app, and copies a status to Movies/Prime Media Status or Pictures/Prime Media Status only when you tap save. No extra storage permission is needed.

Only save content you have the right to keep, and respect the privacy and copyright of the people who posted it. Prime Media is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WhatsApp.

Make video smaller

This tool re-encodes a video on your phone into a smaller copy (for example 480p or 240p). Nothing is uploaded. Your original video is never changed or deleted by this tool. The copy is a new file saved in Movies/Prime Media. The “Delete original” button on the result notification and in the list only deletes the original if you press it and then confirm in Android’s own delete dialog. While it works, a notification with a Cancel button is shown. The app also keeps a small on-device note of how large each original was, so it can show how much space you saved.

Cast to TV

When you tap Cast, Prime Media looks for Google Cast devices on the Wi-Fi network you are connected to, and sends them only the video you picked, directly from your phone over that local network. Some TVs cannot play certain formats (for example HEVC or MKV). If you choose “Convert & cast”, Prime Media makes a temporary TV-friendly copy in the app’s private cache folder; it is deleted when you stop casting. Nothing is sent to the developer.

Where files are saved

  • Audio extracted from a video: Music/Prime Media
  • Files downloaded from Dropbox or your servers: Download/Prime Media (Dropbox files you only play are streamed and not saved)
  • Cut clips and smaller copies made with “Make video smaller”: Movies/Prime Media
  • Saved statuses: Movies/Prime Media Status and Pictures/Prime Media Status
  • On Android 11 and newer, videos you delete wait in the system Recycle Bin for 30 days before they are removed.

Security

The vault file list, saved server passwords and the Dropbox login are encrypted on your device, and vault files are kept in the app’s private storage that other apps cannot read. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we do not send your media to any server of our own.

Children

Prime Media is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and rights

  • Change or withdraw any permission in Android Settings at any time.
  • Clear watch history from Me → Clear Watch History.
  • Sign out of Dropbox and remove saved servers inside the app.
  • Ask us to delete your PIN-recovery email account (see PIN recovery).
  • Uninstall the app to remove all of its on-device data.

Apart from the optional recovery email described above, the developer holds no personal data about you. If you want that email deleted, or have any other request, contact us and we will help.
