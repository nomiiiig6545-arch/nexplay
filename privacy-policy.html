Summary

In short: your videos, music and photos stay on your phone. NexPlay has no user accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer does not collect, sell or share your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains what the NexPlay Android app (package name com.novaplay.videoplayer) accesses, how it uses that access, and the choices you have. It applies to the app and to this website.

What NexPlay accesses and why

NexPlay asks for the following Android permissions. You can withdraw any of them at any time in Android Settings → Apps → NexPlay → Permissions.

PermissionWhy it is needed
Photos and videos (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO; READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on Android 12 and older)To list and play your videos and show your photos. On Android 14 and newer you may choose to share only selected photos and videos (READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED).
Music and audio (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO)To list and play your songs.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)To show the playback notification and its controls.
Foreground service – media playback; Wake lockTo keep music playing when the screen is off or the app is in the background.
Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) – optionalOnly for the live music visualizer, which reads the spectrum of the app’s own audio output. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent. If you say no, the visualizer still animates.
Modify audio settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS)To use the equalizer and the volume boost.
Modify system settings (WRITE_SETTINGS) – optionalOnly when you choose “Set as ringtone” for a song.
Internet (INTERNET)Only for features you start yourself: Dropbox Cloud Drive, FTP/FTPS/SFTP/SMB servers you add, and the optional Gemini photo enhancement.

NexPlay does not request “All files access” (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE), location, contacts, camera, SMS or phone permissions, and it cannot draw over other apps.

Information we collect

None. NexPlay does not collect, sell, rent or share personal information, and it contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. The developer runs no server that receives your files or usage.

The following stays only on your phone and is removed when you uninstall the app: your settings and theme, watch history and “continue watching” positions, favorites, playlists, saved server details, and the vault.

How your media is used

Your videos, music and photos are read on your device only to list, play, edit and display them. Thumbnails and temporary files are created on your device. Files opened with “Open with” from another app are played directly and are not copied or uploaded.

Internet use and third-party services

NexPlay uses the internet only for things you start yourself. When you use them, the relevant third party receives data under its own terms and privacy policy:

  • Cloud Drive (Dropbox): when you sign in, NexPlay talks to Dropbox to list and download your files. Access is read-only. The login token is stored encrypted on your phone and you can sign out at any time. See Dropbox’s privacy policy.
  • Networks (FTP, FTPS, SFTP, SMB): NexPlay connects only to servers you add. Server details, including passwords, are stored encrypted on your phone. Data goes directly between your phone and your server.
  • Optional photo enhancement (Google Gemini): if you choose to use it, the photo you select is sent to Google’s Gemini service using your own API key. It is not used unless you start it. See Google’s privacy policy.

Private Folder (vault)

Files you move into the vault are encrypted on your phone and protected by your PIN or fingerprint. Because the keys are on your device, the developer cannot see vault files and cannot recover your PIN. If you forget the PIN and the recovery answer, vault files cannot be opened. Keep a backup of anything important.

Status Saver

When you tap WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business, Android’s own folder picker opens and you choose that app’s status folder. NexPlay reads only that folder, plays statuses inside the app, and copies a status to Movies/NexPlay Status or Pictures/NexPlay Status only when you tap save. No extra storage permission is needed.

Only save content you have the right to keep, and respect the privacy and copyright of the people who posted it. NexPlay is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WhatsApp.

Where files are saved

  • Audio extracted from a video: Music/NexPlay
  • Files downloaded from Dropbox or your servers: Download/NexPlay
  • Saved statuses: Movies/NexPlay Status and Pictures/NexPlay Status
  • On Android 11 and newer, videos you delete wait in the system Recycle Bin for 30 days before they are removed.

Security

Vault files, saved server passwords and the Dropbox login are encrypted on your device. No method of storage is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we do not transmit your data to any server of our own.

Children

NexPlay is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and rights

  • Change or withdraw any permission in Android Settings at any time.
  • Clear watch history from Me → Clear Watch History.
  • Sign out of Dropbox and remove saved servers inside the app.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all of its on-device data.

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or delete on our side. If you believe otherwise, contact us and we will help.

Changes to this policy
